How to interpret the comparison

One operator has sixteen 15-minute slots. Advance requests take the earliest open slots, skipping evenly spaced walk-in holds. An unbooked slot or no-show can serve the oldest waiting walk-in. Walk-ins arrive at slot boundaries with Poisson demand and leave after at most 30 minutes of waiting or at closing. Each policy sees identical arrivals and attendance draws.

Advance access counts accepted bookings divided by all advance requests, including people who later miss their visit. Walk-in service counts completed walk-in visits divided by walk-in arrivals. The 90th-percentile wait covers served walk-ins only. A dash means there were no eligible observations. The suggested policy maximizes visits subject to both access floors. Ties favor fewer holds.
